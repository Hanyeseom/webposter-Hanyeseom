Max Bill 1908–1994

Swiss designer. Used geometric forms and mathe­matical proportions to create visual order.

Emil Ruder 1914–1970

Swiss typographer. Emphasized readability and the balance of form and function.

Armin Hofmann 1920–2020

Swiss graphic designer. Explored visual expression through point, line, plane, and contrast.

Josef Müller-Brockmann 1914–1996

Swiss graphic designer. Developed systematic grid layouts and objective visual commu­nication.
